# Code of Conduct

## Community standard

People contributing to UETD and uef-gen should make participation welcoming and
constructive for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and
technical viewpoints. Treat others with respect. Give specific, evidence-based
technical feedback and discuss the work rather than attacking the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized
comments, threats, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained personal
attacks, and publishing another person's private information without consent.
Do not disrupt project spaces or retaliate against someone who raises a concern.

## Where it applies

This policy applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any
official project space or event. Project maintainers may apply it to conduct
that materially affects the safety or participation of the community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to the project maintainers using the contact method
listed in the repository's GitHub **About** section. Do not report conduct
concerns in a public issue or pull request. Maintainers will review reports,
protect confidentiality as far as practical, and may remove content, issue a
warning, restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant depending on
the circumstances. Retaliation for a good-faith report is not acceptable.

Before opening public participation, the maintainers will ensure that the
repository has a monitored private contact route and identify who handles
reports when the primary maintainer is involved.
